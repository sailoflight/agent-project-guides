# Publication privacy

## Publication activity timestamps

Author, committer and unsigned annotated tagger dates in this publication are disclosed synthetic UTC counters, not real development, release, work or sleep times. Original records remain private. Commit ancestry and preexisting messages, source/execution identifiers and technical timestamp semantics are preserved. Tag targets refer to the corresponding rewritten commits; tag annotations retain their prior bytes and append this disclosure. Source code, licenses and runtime behavior are preserved; a selected CI scheduling comment uses neutral UTC wording while its actual cron and job configuration remain unchanged.

These metadata changes do not clear every report, date-only record, timestamp-bearing provenance reference, screenshot, hosting event, cache or external copy. Ordinary deployment paths, device models/counts and generic protocol/example timestamps are not cleanup targets.
